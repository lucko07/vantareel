# Sales Call Script

## Opening
- "Thanks for joining. I want to understand your current workflow, then map a practical short-form plan if there is a fit."

## Discovery Questions
- How often do you publish long-form content?
- Are you currently repurposing it into shorts?
- What is your biggest bottleneck?
- What platforms matter most to you?
- What would more consistent short-form output help you achieve?

## Pain Questions
- What happens when clips are delayed?
- How much internal time is currently spent on editing?
- Where do quality issues show up most often?

## Current Workflow Questions
- Who handles clipping, editing, and captioning today?
- How are revisions managed right now?
- Do you have a repeatable delivery structure?

## Volume Questions
- How many long videos per month do you publish?
- What short-form volume would feel meaningful over 30 days?

## Offer Positioning
- One long video can create many clips.
- You do not need to record more.
- VantaReel handles editing, captions, and packaging.
- You receive ready-to-post content weekly.

## Pricing Presentation
- Starter: $750/mo for 12 clips.
- Growth: $1,500/mo for 30 clips.
- Authority: $3,000/mo for 60 clips.

## Objection Handling Prompts
- "Totally fair. Want to compare this against your current time and output cost?"
- "If we removed the editing bottleneck, what would that unlock for your team?"

## Close Questions
- "Do you want to start with a monthly plan this week?"
- "Which plan matches your current content volume?"

## Next Steps
- Confirm plan tier.
- Send onboarding form.
- Collect source links and brand references.
- Start first production cycle.
