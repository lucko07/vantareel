# Objection Handling

## "This is too expensive"
- Reframe against lost consistency, team time, and missed reach.
- Offer starter tier to reduce entry risk.

## "We already have an editor"
- Position VantaReel as specialized short-form throughput support.
- Offer collaboration model with existing editor.

## "We do not have time"
- Clarify low client lift: submit source content and approve notes.
- Emphasize done-for-you weekly delivery.

## "We do not post enough long-form"
- Start with existing archive content plus current releases.
- Use lower volume tier while recording cadence improves.

## "Not sure short-form works for us"
- Offer free content audit and sample transformation.
- Set test period with consistent publishing window.

## "I need to think about it"
- Confirm exact blocker.
- Offer a follow-up date with a clear decision framework.
