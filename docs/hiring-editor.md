# Hiring Spec: Short-Form Editor

## Role Summary
Edit long-form source footage into high-retention short-form clips for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.

## Required Skills
- Strong story and hook judgment.
- Fast-paced short-form editing.
- Clean caption styling.

## Software Familiarity
- Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, or CapCut Desktop.
- Basic audio leveling and cleanup.

## Captioning Standards
- Readable mobile-first sizing.
- Accurate transcription and line timing.

## Test Task
Complete the assignment in `docs/editor-test-task.md`.

## Expected Turnaround
- Draft batch within agreed weekly windows.
- Revisions within 1 to 3 business days.

## Communication Expectations
- Daily async updates during active batches.
- Early escalation of blockers.
