# Revision Policy

## Included
- One revision round per weekly batch.
- Scope: hooks, trim points, captions, text overlays, and sequencing adjustments.

## Not Included
- Full concept reset after approval.
- New clip requests outside agreed batch volume.

## Turnaround
- Standard revision completion target: 1 to 3 business days.
- Revision requests should be consolidated into one message per batch.
