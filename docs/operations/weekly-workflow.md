# Weekly Workflow

## Monday
- Intake new source videos and priority notes.
- Confirm clip target count by plan.

## Tuesday
- Select moments and build first-pass edits.

## Wednesday
- Caption, style, and pacing polish.
- Internal QA pass.

## Thursday
- Submit client drafts or revisions.

## Friday
- Deliver final clips and update tracker.
- Prepare next-week intake list.
