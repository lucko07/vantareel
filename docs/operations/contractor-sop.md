# Contractor SOP

## Objective
Deliver short-form clips that are hook-first, brand-aligned, and ready to post across TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.

## 1. Review Long-Form Content
- Watch full source content at 1x first pass.
- Mark timestamps with strongest emotional or practical value.
- Prioritize moments with clear, standalone payoff.

## 2. Identify Clip-Worthy Moments
- Look for opinions, frameworks, mistakes, contrarian points, and mini stories.
- Prioritize segments that can hook within 1 to 2 seconds.
- Avoid sections that require too much context.

## 3. Hook Best Practices
- Start with tension, curiosity, or specific result.
- Remove slow intros and setup filler.
- Keep opening line concise and outcome-focused.

## 4. Caption Styling Rules
- Large, readable captions with consistent hierarchy.
- Highlight 1 to 3 key words only when it adds clarity.
- Keep safe-zone spacing for platform UI overlays.

## 5. Export Settings
- Primary export: 1080x1920 (9:16), H.264, 20-40 Mbps.
- Frame rate: match source unless instructed otherwise.
- Audio: normalized and free of clipping.

## 6. Filename Rules
- `{Client}-{YYYYMMDD}-{Topic}-{ClipNumber}-{Format}.mp4`
- Example: `Acme-20260308-LeadGen-Clip03-916.mp4`

## 7. Delivery Folder Structure
- Use the standard template in `docs/operations/delivery-folder-template.md`.

## 8. QA Checklist
- Hook is clear in first 2 seconds.
- Captions are accurate and readable.
- Visual pacing has no dead air.
- Branded elements follow client style.
- Export matches required format and naming.
