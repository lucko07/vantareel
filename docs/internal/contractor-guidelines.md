# Contractor Guidelines

## What Editors Are Expected To Do
- Turn long-form source into polished short-form clips.
- Follow hook-first structure and caption standards.
- Deliver files in approved naming and folder formats.

## Turnaround
- Respect weekly deadlines.
- Flag blockers before they affect delivery windows.

## Communication Expectations
- Keep updates concise and actionable.
- Submit revision status clearly by clip ID.

## QC Standards
- Pass all checks in `docs/operations/clip-quality-standards.md`.

## Revisions Allowed
- One revision round per batch unless otherwise scoped.
